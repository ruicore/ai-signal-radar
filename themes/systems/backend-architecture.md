## Notable Recurring Signals

- Agent backends need explicit control layers for identity, policy, command execution, memory, telemetry, cost attribution, and review gates.
- Human policy and architecture, agent execution, and deterministic evidence are recurring layers of sustainable agent systems.
- Production AI backends increasingly isolate probabilistic intelligence behind explicit latency, behavior, environment, evidence, and admission boundaries.
